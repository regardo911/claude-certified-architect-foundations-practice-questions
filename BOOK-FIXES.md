# Book fixes

The book is already printed. Where a sheet can't follow it exactly, the sheet changes it, and the change is listed here.

## Chapter 12: GO and WAIT can both be true

The GO line says: "every domain is at or above 75% on your hardest mock so far (Mock 3 now, or a later Appendix A mock after the finishing plan)". The WAIT line says: "any domain is below 75% on Mock 3".

You never retake the full Mock 3. The 7-day plan sits Mock 4 ("A fresh full mock from Appendix A (Mock 4)") and, without the official practice exam, Mock 5. So a domain under 75% on Mock 3 that clears it on Mock 4 meets the GO line and the WAIT line. Appendix B's GO line says "on your hardest mock" and names no mock.

The [verdict sheet](chapters/12-verdict/readiness-verdict.md) says "on your hardest mock so far" in both lines.

## Appendix B: the readiness bands skip 79% and 89%

Appendix B prints the bands as "Below ~70%", "~70 to 78%", "~80 to 88%" and "~90% and up". A 79% or an 89% lands in none of them.

The [verdict sheet](chapters/12-verdict/readiness-verdict.md) uses ~70 to 79% and ~80 to 89%, keeping the book's "~" and its meaning for each band.

## Chapter 3: three rules for which domain to drill first

BUILD STEP, step 3: "The biggest gap is the domain you attack first." The closing line of the build section: "one domain at a time, starting with the heaviest." The Context Management explanations: if you missed the silent-failure question, "start your drilling here even if a bigger domain scored lower."

The [readiness map](chapters/03-diagnostic/readiness-map.md) follows the BUILD STEP: rank by gap, biggest first. It adds a Rank column and a "Drill first" line the book's table doesn't print.
