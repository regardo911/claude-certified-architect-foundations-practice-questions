# Chapters 9 to 11 and Appendix A: six mocks

Six full mocks, each 60 questions in 120 minutes, closed-book. The questions and the keys are in the book. This folder covers all six sittings.

| Mock | Questions and key | Level |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chapter 9 | The first full simulation |
| 2 | Chapter 10 | A notch harder |
| 3 | Chapter 11 | The hardest of the three |
| 4 | Appendix A | Baseline |
| 5 | Appendix A | A notch harder |
| 6 | Appendix A | The hardest in the book |

## Each sitting uses two sheets

Print a fresh [answer-sheet.pdf](answer-sheet.pdf) every time; it works for any of the six. Circle the mock number, write your start time, and go. It lists question numbers only. Each question's domain is in the book's key.

[score-by-domain.pdf](score-by-domain.pdf) is for after you mark. It has one page per mock, listing which question numbers belong to which domain in that mock's key. Count your misses in each row and work out each domain's score. Print the whole file once. Keep it face down while the clock runs.

## Then the trend

Copy the five scores from every sitting into the six-mock table on [mock-trend.pdf](mock-trend.pdf). After Mock 2, fill the side-by-side table on the same sheet as well. Chapter 10's CHECKPOINT: "a per-domain delta against Mock 1, so you can name your one or two stuck domains exactly." The book calls those stuck domains "your remaining-prep target".

## Mock 3 adds the trap checklist

After you mark Mock 3, tick the trap behind each miss on [trap-checklist.pdf](trap-checklist.pdf). Use the matrix printed in Chapter 11 or the seven traps from Chapter 2. Then write your checklist at the bottom, most frequent trap first, with the tiebreak you missed beside each. It goes in your pocket on exam morning.

Every miss from every mock also gets a line in the [miss-log](../02-scorecard/scorecard-and-miss-log.md), domain tag from the key included. Chapter 12's [7-day plan](../12-verdict/seven-day-plan.md) uses Mock 4, and Mock 5 if you can't sit the official practice exam.
