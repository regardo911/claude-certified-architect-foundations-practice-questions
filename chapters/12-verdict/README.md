# Chapter 12: the verdict

Lay out your scorecard, your readiness map, your mock trend and your trap checklist. Then fill [readiness-verdict.pdf](readiness-verdict.pdf). It's done when the chapter's CHECKPOINT is true: "every domain row filled, a trend marked, and a single word written at the bottom: GO or WAIT."

Under the verdict are the book's raw-score bands from Appendix B. They tell you what the raw score on your hardest mock means. The printed table skipped 79% and 89%; the sheet doesn't. [BOOK-FIXES.md](../../BOOK-FIXES.md) explains that and the one-line change to the WAIT rule.

## Print two copies

You'll use the second one if the first says WAIT, or says GO with a thin margin. In that case run [seven-day-plan.pdf](seven-day-plan.pdf). It uses Mock 4 from Appendix A, and Mock 5 if you can't sit the official practice exam. Then fill the second verdict. If it's GO now, book it.

## The card for the room

The [exam-day card](exam-day-card.pdf) is the one sheet that's mostly the book's own words, cut down to fit one page. Chapter 12 says to print it or copy it onto an index card. Run the checklist the night before; read it once more on the morning of.
