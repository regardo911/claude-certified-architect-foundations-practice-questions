# Exam-day card

**The clock.** 120 minutes, 60 questions, two minutes each. Be at question 30 around the one-hour mark.

**The decision rule, every question.** Read the full stem before you look at the options. Name the failure or the constraint. Eliminate options by the named trap each one runs. Pick the calibrated middle.

**The tiebreak.** When two answers both look right, the answer is in the stem: find the one constraint that separates them. Your trap-patterns matrix from Chapter 11 is that list.

**Flag and move.** Anything over two minutes: best guess, flag it, move. Come back with the time you have left.

**Stack the deck on the proctoring.** One monitor, the others disconnected. A clear desk: no phone, no notes, no second screen. Alone in the room. Eyes on the screen while you think. No tab switching. Results are reported to take days.

## The night before

- [ ] Test the setup end to end: webcam, internet, one monitor.
- [ ] Run the proctoring checklist above.
- [ ] Stop studying.

## The morning of

- [ ] Read your trap checklist once, slowly.
- [ ] Re-read this card: the clock and the rule for every question.
- [ ] Eat something.
